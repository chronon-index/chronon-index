## The headline is no longer the sign — it is the baseline policy

v1 reported "2026 mortality runs below baseline, cumulative −8 ppm."
That number is REAL arithmetic and WRONG inference: the kk-linear
2015–2019 baseline extrapolates nine years from its window centre, and
the slope term (~+3.7% of expected deaths) is three times larger than
the measured deficit. The sensitivity table (reporting-set coverage,
regenerated by the module):

| baseline policy | cum 2026 ppm of S | excl. edge week |
|---|---|---|
| kk-linear 2015–2019 (current registered policy) | −9.88 | −15.35 |
| mean 2015–2019 | +19.18 | — |
| kk-linear 2017–2019 | +28.45 | — |
| kk-linear 2016–2019 | −87.38 | — |
| kk-linear 2015–2018 | −26.63 | — |

Same method on prior years: 2024 ≈ −20 ppm, 2025 ≈ −5 ppm — the
"deficit" is window drift, not news about 2026. **The decision order
for any activation proposal is therefore: (1) baseline policy for the
live-burn era — a governed, registered choice with its own version
bump — and only then (2) the sign rule.**
